## Logo direction

"Perennial" suggests longevity, growth, evergreen. Palette is forest/sage.

Possible visual directions:
- Stylized leaf or sprout
- Tree growing from a chart line
- Geometric "P" with leaf accent

Avoid: dollar signs, candlesticks, generic finance clichés.
