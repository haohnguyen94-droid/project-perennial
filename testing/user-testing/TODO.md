## Recruitment

Target: **5–8 testers** across personas:
- 2–3 matching **Alex** (young pro, 22–30, never actively invested)
- 2–3 matching **Maria** (college student, 18–22, financially curious)
- 2–3 matching **David** (career changer, 28–40, tried apps unsuccessfully)

Screen out: experienced investors, finance professionals, friends who know the project.

---

## Testing script (30–45 min)

1. **Intro (3 min)** — consent, recording, "testing the app not you"
2. **Background (5 min)** — current knowledge, apps used
3. **Task 1: Onboarding (10 min)** — Get Started → Choose Interest → Confirmation → Homepage, narrate thoughts
4. **Task 2: Find investment via event (10 min)** — "Find something interesting in tech and tell me what you'd consider investing in and why" → should naturally use Insight + Event Detail + Company Detail
5. **Task 3: Research a company (10 min)** — "Look up [company] and tell me whether you'd invest" → should use Search Bar / Affordable & Growing / Popular & Stable + Company Detail
6. **Task 4: Watchlist (5 min)** — "Save 2 companies for later. Then remove one."
7. **Debrief (5 min)** — confusing? clear? would use this?

---

## What to look for

- Do users understand the **Event Impact Flow chart**? (the differentiator)
- Do they trust the **Confident Score**? Do they ask "why" before we point at the Breakdown?
- Do they grasp the difference between **Affordable & Growing** and **Popular & Stable** without explanation?
- Which **Sentiment Pulse tab** (News / Social / Analyst) feels most relevant to them?
- Is the **Watchlist empty state** with topic chips helpful?
- Do they feel empowered or intimidated?
